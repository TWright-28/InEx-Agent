\subsection{Ground-Truth Construction for Classifier Validation}
\label{sec:ground-truth}

To evaluate the automatic classifier we constructed a manually labelled
gold-standard set of 30 issues, balanced across the three target categories
(10 \emph{Intrinsic}, 10 \emph{Extrinsic}, 10 \emph{Not-a-Bug}). The issues were
sampled from the larger collected corpus and each was labelled by hand,
independently of the model. For every issue the annotator read the title and
body, the maintainer discussion, and---decisively---the \emph{resolving pull
request or commit}. The resolution is the strongest available signal for the
root cause: it reveals whether the defect lay in the project's own code, in an
external dependency or toolchain, or whether the report was not a production
defect at all. Each issue was then assigned exactly one label according to the
definitions in Table~\ref{tab:labels}, and the supporting evidence was recorded
as a short rationale. Representative examples are shown in
Table~\ref{tab:groundtruth}.

\begin{table}[t]
  \centering
  \caption{Classification schema used by the human annotator to develop the
           ground-truth labels.}
  \label{tab:labels}
  \begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}lX@{}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Label} & \textbf{Definition (root cause of the issue)} \\
    \midrule
    Intrinsic  & Root cause is \emph{inside} the repository---a logic error,
                 missing validation, or a regression from the project's own code. \\
    Extrinsic  & Root cause is \emph{outside} the repository---a dependency,
                 runtime, or toolchain change the project had to adapt to. \\
    Not-a-Bug  & Not a defect in production code---usage questions,
                 documentation, feature requests, test/CI issues, or user
                 misconfiguration. \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\subsubsection{Validation result}

Against this ground truth the classifier agreed on 27 of the 30 issues
(90\,\% overall). Per-class agreement is reported in Table~\ref{tab:agreement}.
All three disagreements were of the same kind: the classifier assigned
\emph{Not-a-Bug} to an issue the annotator judged a genuine defect
(ember-cli\#6110, webpack\#2616, and gatsby\#20945), indicating a conservative
bias toward \emph{Not-a-Bug} when the causal evidence is indirect. No
\emph{Intrinsic}/\emph{Extrinsic} confusions occurred.

  \centering
  \caption{Per-class agreement between the classifier and the human ground
